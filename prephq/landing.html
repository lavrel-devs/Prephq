FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you'd want to know before you start.

What is PrepHQ?
PrepHQ is an AI-powered exam preparation platform built specifically for Nigerian university students. It generates unlimited, course-specific practice questions so you can study the pattern behind exam questions, not just memorize past papers.
How does the AI work?
You pick a course, a topic, and how many questions you want. The AI generates a fresh set of questions tailored to that topic, then gives you step-by-step feedback on each answer — not just whether you got it right, but why.
What courses are supported?
PrepHQ covers a growing list of courses across Nigerian university curricula. If your exact course isn't listed yet, you can also upload your own notes or course outline and the AI will study it directly.
Do I need to pay to use it?
No. PrepHQ has a free tier with limited daily questions so you can try it before committing to anything. Pro and Lifetime plans unlock unlimited AI questions and extra features.
How do credits work?
Each AI-generated quiz costs a small number of credits. Free accounts get a limited daily allowance; Pro and Lifetime plans include unlimited generation, so credits are never a concern.
Can I use it on my phone?
Yes. PrepHQ is fully responsive and built to work smoothly on mobile — no app store download required, just open it in your browser.
