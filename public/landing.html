Features

What you can do with PrepHQ.

Everything is built to help you go from “I read it” to “I can answer it in the exam.”

Practise your courses

Pick the courses you're taking this semester and answer questions. You see straight away if you got it right, and why.

Quizzes made for you

Choose any topic and the app makes a new quiz on it, so you learn the idea instead of memorising one paper.

Ask a question

Stuck on something? Type your question and get a clear, step-by-step explanation in simple words.

Notes & flashcards

Short notes and flashcards to revise from, plus a list of the topics you keep getting wrong.

Study with friends

Join contests, see the leaderboard, and study together with friends in shared rooms.

Maths & chemistry that display properly

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you'd want to know before you start.

How do I log in?

Tap Log in at the top of this page. Type your matric number and the password you chose when you signed up, then tap Sign In. If you haven't signed up yet, tap Sign up free first.

What do I need to sign up?

Your full name, your matric number, a phone number, and a password you'll remember. You don't need a card or any payment.

What is PrepHQ?

PrepHQ is a study app for Nigerian university students. You practise questions for your courses, take quizzes, and see which topics need more work.

Do I need a code or an invitation?

No. Sign-up is free and open to everyone. Create an account and start practising straight away.

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan gives you a daily allowance of questions, AI quizzes and AI chat messages. Upgrade only if you want more.

How do I upgrade?

Choose a plan from your profile and message us on WhatsApp. We activate your plan from our side, so there's no card or online payment step.

I forgot my password. What now?

On the Log in page, tap “Forgot password”. Your request goes to an admin on WhatsApp, who helps you get back in.

Do I need to download anything?

No. PrepHQ works in your phone's browser, the same way you open any website. You can also add it to your home screen if you like.
