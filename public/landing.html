FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you'd want to know before you start.

What is PrepHQ?

PrepHQ is an AI-powered exam preparation platform for Nigerian university students. You practise course questions, generate AI quizzes and see which topics need work.

Do I need a code to sign up?

No. Sign-up is free and open to everyone. Create an account and start practising straight away.

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan gives you a daily allowance of questions, AI quizzes and AI chat messages. Upgrade only if you want more.

How do I upgrade?

Choose a plan from your profile and message us on WhatsApp. We activate your plan from our side, so there's no card or online payment step.

I forgot my password. What now?

Use “Forgot password” on the login page. Your request goes to an admin on WhatsApp, who resets your access manually.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. PrepHQ is built for phones first, and there's nothing to download. Just open it in your browser.
