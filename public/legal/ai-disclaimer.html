AI Disclaimer

Effective 30 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-30
In short: AI writes questions, explains answers and marks written work on Wreddi. It can make mistakes, its marks are only a guide, and it is no substitute for your lecturer's material. Your text is sent to an AI provider, and AI-written questions may be reviewed and added to the bank.

1. About this notice

Wreddi uses artificial intelligence (AI) in several features. This notice explains what that means for you. It is part of our Terms & Conditions. By using the AI features you accept it.

2. Where AI is used

  • AI quizzes: questions and answer choices written by AI.
  • Written (theory) exams: questions, marking schemes and model answers written by AI (and sometimes questions our team has reviewed), and your answers marked by AI.
  • Study assistant: chat answers and explanations, including questions you ask by voice.
  • Study aids: notes, study guides and plans written or suggested by AI.

3. AI can be wrong

  • AI output may be inaccurate, incomplete, out of date, biased, or not match your lecturer's notes or your course outline. Questions may have the wrong answer marked, and explanations may contain errors.
  • Marks given by AI are only an indication to help you practise. They are not an official grade, are not checked by a lecturer, and may be stricter or more generous than a real examiner.
  • Always cross-check important facts with your lecturer's material and recommended textbooks. If a question or answer looks wrong, tell us using the "report" option or the Support option inside the app.

4. Not professional advice

AI output is for study practice only. It is not academic, legal, medical, financial or other professional advice, and Wreddi does not guarantee any result in any examination.

5. Your data and the AI

When you use an AI feature, the text you submit is sent to our AI provider to produce the result (see "AI processing" in the Privacy Policy). Do not enter other people's personal information, passwords, or confidential or restricted documents. Voice questions are turned into text by your browser's speech service before they reach us.

6. Review of AI-written questions

Questions the AI writes for you may be added to a review queue. Our team may approve, edit or reject them. Approved questions may be added to the Wreddi question banks and shown to other students without any link to you. Questions generated from notes you paste in yourself are not sent for review.

7. Using AI responsibly

Use AI to learn, not to cheat. Follow your school's academic integrity rules, and read our Acceptable Use Policy.
