Acceptable Use Policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-30
In short: play fair (one account, no cheating or scraping), be respectful, keep it legal, and don't use PrepHQ in a real exam where outside help isn't allowed. Breaking the rules can lead to removed rewards or a suspended account.

On this page

  1. Why these rules exist
  2. Don't cheat the system
  3. Be respectful
  4. Keep it legal and safe
  5. Reporting and enforcement

1. Why these rules exist

PrepHQ works because students trust it and use it fairly. This Acceptable Use Policy is part of our Terms & Conditions. By using the Service you agree to follow it.

2. Don't cheat the system

  • Do not create multiple accounts, use someone else's account or matric number, or share or sell your account or activation codes.
  • Do not exploit bugs, farm or fake credits, referrals, streaks, rewards, contests or leaderboard positions, or get around daily limits, plans or the paywall.
  • Do not use scripts, bots, scrapers or automated tools on the Service, or copy or bulk-download questions, explanations or marking schemes.
  • Do not try to trick the AI into ignoring its instructions, revealing hidden material (for example a marking scheme or model answers before you submit) or awarding marks you have not earned.

3. Be respectful

  • No harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats, sexual content, or content that targets a person or group because of tribe, religion, gender, disability or any other characteristic.
  • No impersonation. Do not use a username or profile that pretends to be someone else, or an admin or staff member.
  • Keep study rooms and chats about studying. No spam, advertising, scams, chain messages or unrelated promotion.

4. Keep it legal and safe

  • Do not post or upload anything unlawful, defamatory, or that breaks someone's copyright, privacy or other rights (for example a lecturer's protected material that you have no permission to share).
  • Do not share other people's personal details, and do not put passwords, ID numbers or bank details into the app or the AI features.
  • Do not attack, probe, overload or interfere with the Service, its servers, other users' accounts or our security. If you find a security problem, please report it to us instead of using it.
  • Do not use PrepHQ in a way that breaks any law or your school's rules. In particular, do not use the Service during a real examination or assessment where outside help is not allowed. You are responsible for following your institution's academic integrity rules.

5. Reporting and enforcement

If you see something that breaks these rules, or a question that is wrong, report it through the app or contact us through the Support option inside the app. If we find that these rules have been broken, we may remove content, take away credits, rewards or leaderboard results gained unfairly, limit features, suspend or end the account, and, where the law requires or it is necessary to protect people, report the matter to the relevant authorities. Credits and paid time on an account that is ended for breaking these rules are not refunded.
