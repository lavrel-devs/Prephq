Cookies & Storage Notice

Effective 30 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-30
In short: Wreddi uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and remember your settings. No advertising cookies and no tracking. Some outside providers (fonts, icons) can see your IP address when your device loads their files.

On this page

  1. What this notice covers
  2. What we store on your device
  3. Outside services your browser contacts
  4. Your choices

1. What this notice covers

This notice explains what Wreddi stores on your device and which outside services your browser contacts when you use the app. By using Wreddi you agree to this. Wreddi does not set advertising cookies or use third-party tracking or advertising technology.

2. What we store on your device

We use your browser's local storage (similar to cookies but kept only in your browser) for the following. All of it is needed to make the Service work or to remember your choices.

ItemPurposeHow long
Sign-in session (phq_session)Keeps you logged in: your account reference and the tokens that prove you are signed inUntil you log out, the session expires, or you clear your browser data
Device identifier (phq_device_fp)Lets us recognise a new device logging in to your account, for securityUntil you clear your browser data
Theme (phq_theme)Remembers light or dark modeUntil you clear your browser data
Return link (phq_next)Remembers a quiz or challenge link you opened before signing in, so we can take you back to itUp to 24 hours
Voice and exam drafts (phq_voice, phq_wx_…)Remembers your voice settings and saves your written-exam answers as you type so you don't lose themSettings until cleared; exam drafts until you submit
Cached app dataSpeeds up the app and keeps recent questions and progress available (for example your question bank and daily goal)Until updated or cleared

3. Outside services your browser contacts

To display Wreddi, your browser loads resources from other providers, such as fonts (Google Fonts), icons and code libraries (for example from cdnjs), and the real-time connection library used by study rooms. These providers can see your IP address and browser details when your device requests those files, under their own privacy policies. If you choose to open a WhatsApp link, WhatsApp is contacted.

4. Your choices

  • You can clear Wreddi's stored data any time in your browser settings (clearing site data). You will be logged out and your saved drafts and preferences will be reset.
  • Blocking local storage will stop you from logging in, because the Service needs it to keep your session.
  • If we ever add analytics or other non-essential technologies, we will update this notice first.
